Beyond the recordings

The added proofs make assumptions explicit; the original examples separate gross loss from insurer payment; the numerical laboratory checks finite cases against exact calculations. The concentration appendix supplies an independently authored bridge to approximation theory. Research and source notes distinguish these additions from lecture statements.

The reader works offline with bundled mathematics, search, text controls, code copying and printable solutions. Recordings and external references require an internet connection.
